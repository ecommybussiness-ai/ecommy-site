How to create an Amazon seller account in India.

Start with preparation, clear product information and a plan for your first catalogue.

Creating an Amazon seller account in India is easy to start but needs thoughtful preparation to run well. The strongest launch is not just a completed registration—it is a ready-to-sell catalogue, accurate operations information and a simple plan for the first month.

What to prepare before registration

Begin by deciding which products, variants and brands you will actually launch first. Keep business contact information, payout details and any tax or compliance documents that apply to your seller type ready. Marketplace requirements can change by category and business structure, so confirm the current requirements in the Amazon seller registration flow before submitting anything.

Create one master product sheet before you build the catalogue. Include your internal SKU, product name, brand, category, size or colour variants, dimensions, package contents, price, stock quantity and image status. This makes it much easier to manage the launch and future catalogue changes.

Build product information customers can trust

A product page should answer a buyer’s practical questions: what is it, who is it for, what does it include, what size is it and how is it used? Start with a clear title, then use images, bullet points and a description to complete the story. Avoid vague claims or benefits that you cannot support.

For every SKU, check that the main image identifies the item quickly, supporting images show important details and the selected variation matches the image, price and stock. Clear information reduces incorrect orders and gives advertising a stronger page to send visitors to.

Set up a launch-ready catalogue

  • Use an internal SKU system so inventory and catalogue updates remain traceable.
  • Keep all variant names consistent—for example, do not mix “Large”, “L” and “XL size” in one product family.
  • Prepare measurements and package contents before filling product attributes.
  • Check the live page on both desktop and mobile after publishing.
  • Keep saleable inventory available for products you plan to promote.

Make a simple first-30-days plan

In week one, focus on accuracy: resolve listing warnings, confirm pricing and ensure each product is showing the intended details. In week two, review traffic and conversion signals. If you advertise, start with a small, trackable set of products rather than trying to promote every listing at once.

During weeks three and four, identify which products attract useful visits and which pages need work. Improve the underlying offer before increasing spend: title clarity, images, price, availability and product information all influence the buyer’s decision after a click.

Common launch mistakes to avoid

Do not copy the same description across unrelated products, publish incomplete variation data or begin advertising while the product is out of stock. Another common mistake is changing price, images, targeting and budgets all at once; that makes it impossible to understand what actually improved performance. Make one meaningful change at a time and record why you made it.

Quick questions

Should I list every product on day one?

Not necessarily. Start with the products for which you have complete images, product data and stock. A smaller, well-built launch catalogue is easier to review and improve.

When should I begin advertising?

Begin only when the advertised listing is accurate, available and ready to convert. Advertising can create visits, but it cannot compensate for missing product information.

Where can I check current requirements?

Use the official Amazon India seller portal during registration, because category, tax and account requirements can change.
